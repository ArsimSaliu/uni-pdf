Good to know

  • Notes save automatically, in this browser only. Use Export → Back up before clearing browser data or switching computers.
  • Export → Editable PDF gives you one file that has both the slides and your notes. Open it here again, on any computer, to keep editing.
  • Opening the same PDF again, even from another folder, brings back its notes.

Study

Every highlight, area or drawing with a note is a card. Tip: write notes as answers, e.g. highlight “PCB” and note “stores process state, saved on context switch”.
