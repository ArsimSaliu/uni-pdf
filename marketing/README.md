# Marketing & launch kit

- **[LAUNCH-PLAN.md](LAUNCH-PLAN.md)**: the pre-launch checklist, a week-by-week launch schedule, campus ideas and directories.
- **[POSTS.md](POSTS.md)**: ready-to-post copy for Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Instagram/TikTok, and emails to your student union and lecturers (English and German).
- **[PRESS-KIT.md](PRESS-KIT.md)**: facts, short descriptions and a list of every image and video.
- **[assets/](assets/)**: printable flyers (EN/DE) with a QR code, Product Hunt gallery images, and square and story images.
- `flyer.html`: the flyer source. Edit it, then run `npm run marketing` to regenerate everything.
